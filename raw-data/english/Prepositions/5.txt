11. Mr. Demmings bought some property ________ the West Coast ________ the early 1990s.

✅ Đáp án: on – in

👉 Hoàn chỉnh câu:

Mr. Demmings bought some property on the West Coast in the early 1990s.

Giải thích:

on the West Coast → coast (bờ biển) dùng on

in the early 1990s → thập niên / giai đoạn thời gian

📌 Mẹo thi:

coast → on | decade → in

12. ________ January, all of the office furniture at Office Works will be ________ sale ________ greatly reduced prices.

✅ Đáp án: In – on – at

👉 Hoàn chỉnh câu:

In January, all of the office furniture at Office Works will be on sale at greatly reduced prices.

Giải thích:

in January → tháng

on sale = đang giảm giá (cụm cố định)

at reduced prices → ở mức giá

📌 Combo cực hay ra TOEIC:

on sale at a reduced price

13. I will arrive ________ Orly Airport ________ Paris ________ around nine o’clock.

✅ Đáp án: at – in – at

👉 Hoàn chỉnh câu:

I will arrive at Orly Airport in Paris at around nine o’clock.

Giải thích:

arrive at + sân bay

in + thành phố

at + giờ cụ thể

📌 Mẹo xếp lớp chuẩn:

at (place nhỏ) → in (place lớn) → at (time)

14. ________ present, there are no job openings ________ the design department, but there may be an opening ________ a month or two.

✅ Đáp án: At – in – in

👉 Hoàn chỉnh câu:

At present, there are no job openings in the design department, but there may be an opening in a month or two.

Giải thích:

at present = hiện tại (cụm cố định)

in the department → bộ phận

in a month or two = trong vòng 1–2 tháng nữa (tương lai)

📌 Bẫy hay gặp:
❌ at a month (sai)

15. Some of the most fashionable and expensive stores ________ the United States are ________ Rodeo Drive ________ Los Angeles.

✅ Đáp án: in – on – in

👉 Hoàn chỉnh câu:

Some of the most fashionable and expensive stores in the United States are on Rodeo Drive in Los Angeles.

Giải thích:

in the United States → quốc gia

on Rodeo Drive → tên đường

in Los Angeles → thành phố

📌 Chuỗi vị trí TOEIC:

in (country) → on (street) → in (city)
