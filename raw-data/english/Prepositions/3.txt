Focus: Completing sentences with prepositions that precede certain prepositional objects.

Directions: Fill in the blanks with the correct prepositions.

1. I met my friend Howard ________ chance ________ the lobby of the Raffles Hotel ________ Singapore.

✅ Đáp án: by – in – in

👉 Hoàn chỉnh câu:

I met my friend Howard by chance in the lobby of the Raffles Hotel in Singapore.

Giải thích:

by chance = tình cờ (cụm cố định)

in the lobby → không gian kín

in Singapore → quốc gia / thành phố lớn

📌 Mẹo thi:

by chance / by accident → học như 1 cụm

2. There’s a phone number ________ the newspaper that we can call ________ more information.

✅ Đáp án: in – for

👉 Hoàn chỉnh câu:

There’s a phone number in the newspaper that we can call for more information.

Giải thích:

in the newspaper = nằm trong nội dung báo

call for information = gọi để xin thông tin

📌 Bẫy hay gặp:
❌ call to information (sai TOEIC)

3. ________ Korea, it is considered bad luck to sign your name ________ a red ink pen.

✅ Đáp án: In – with

👉 Hoàn chỉnh câu:

In Korea, it is considered bad luck to sign your name with a red ink pen.

Giải thích:

In + quốc gia = xét trong phạm vi văn hóa

with + công cụ = dùng cái gì để làm

📌 Mẹo nhớ:

write / sign / cut / open → with + tool

4. The plumber promised me he would be here ________ least ________ three, but he didn’t arrive ________ five.

✅ Đáp án: at – by – until

👉 Hoàn chỉnh câu:

The plumber promised me he would be here at least by three, but he didn’t arrive until five.

Giải thích:

at least = ít nhất

by three = chậm nhất là 3 giờ

until five = mãi đến 5 giờ mới tới

📌 Cặp cực hay ra TOEIC:

by = deadline
until = mốc kết thúc chờ đợi

5. InterSystem’s international sales increased ________ 21% ________ 2000 and 2005.

✅ Đáp án: by – between

👉 Hoàn chỉnh câu:

InterSystem’s international sales increased by 21% between 2000 and 2005.

Giải thích:

increase by + số % = tăng bao nhiêu

between A and B = trong khoảng hai mốc

📌 Bẫy kinh điển:
❌ increased to 21% (nghĩa khác hoàn toàn)
