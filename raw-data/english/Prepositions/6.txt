16. Textiles were made ________ hand ________ the invention of the power loom ________ the nineteenth century.

✅ Đáp án: by – before – in

👉 Hoàn chỉnh câu:

Textiles were made by hand before the invention of the power loom in the nineteenth century.

Giải thích:

by hand = làm thủ công (cụm cố định)

before + danh từ / sự kiện

in the nineteenth century → thế kỷ

📌 Mẹo thi:

by hand ≠ with hand (sai)

17. Her photo appeared ________ the cover of Business Watch, and there was a story about her ________ the magazine as well.

✅ Đáp án: on – in

👉 Hoàn chỉnh câu:

Her photo appeared on the cover of Business Watch, and there was a story about her in the magazine as well.

Giải thích:

on the cover → bề mặt

in the magazine → nội dung bên trong

📌 Cặp rất hay ra TOEIC:

on the cover / in the magazine

18. I commute to work ________ the city ________ train, but my friend always travels there ________ his own car.

✅ Đáp án: from – by – in

👉 Hoàn chỉnh câu:

I commute to work from the city by train, but my friend always travels there in his own car.

Giải thích:

commute from (nơi xuất phát)

by train → phương tiện (không mạo từ)

in his own car → phương tiện có sở hữu

📌 Bẫy kinh điển:

by car ❌ khi có his / my → phải dùng in

19. Every year, people ________ Mexico celebrate their independence from Spain ________ September 16.

✅ Đáp án: in – on

👉 Hoàn chỉnh câu:

Every year, people in Mexico celebrate their independence from Spain on September 16.

Giải thích:

in Mexico → quốc gia

on + ngày cụ thể

📌 Mẹo nhớ:

on + date | in + country

20. ________ 1988 to 1992, he lived ________ a small farm ________ Vermont.

✅ Đáp án: From – on – in

👉 Hoàn chỉnh câu:

From 1988 to 1992, he lived on a small farm in Vermont.

Giải thích:

from … to … → khoảng thời gian

on a farm → địa điểm mở, bề mặt

in Vermont → bang / vùng

📌 Chuỗi vị trí chuẩn TOEIC:

on (farm) → in (state)
