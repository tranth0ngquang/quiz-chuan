6. Mr. Poernomo asked me to meet him ________ his office ________ the third floor ________ two-thirty, so I need to leave ________ a few minutes.

✅ Đáp án: in – on – at – in

👉 Hoàn chỉnh câu:

Mr. Poernomo asked me to meet him in his office on the third floor at two-thirty, so I need to leave in a few minutes.

Giải thích:

in his office → không gian kín

on the third floor → tầng → on

at two-thirty → giờ cụ thể

in a few minutes = trong vài phút nữa (tương lai)

📌 Chuỗi chuẩn TOEIC:

in (room) – on (floor) – at (time)

7. Deborah got ________ trouble ________ her boss for illegally copying software.

✅ Đáp án: in – with

👉 Hoàn chỉnh câu:

Deborah got in trouble with her boss for illegally copying software.

Giải thích:

get in trouble = gặp rắc rối

with someone = với ai đó (người có thẩm quyền)

📌 Cụm phải thuộc lòng:

get in trouble with sb

8. I always shower ________ the morning, but my roommate showers ________ night.

✅ Đáp án: in – at

👉 Hoàn chỉnh câu:

I always shower in the morning, but my roommate showers at night.

Giải thích:

in the morning / afternoon / evening

at night (cụm cố định)

📌 Bẫy rất hay ra:
❌ in night (sai tuyệt đối)

9. Ms. Vu has been living ________ that apartment building ________ 2460 Vine Street ________ September, but her sister has been there ________ several years.

✅ Đáp án: in – at – since – for

👉 Hoàn chỉnh câu:

Ms. Vu has been living in that apartment building at 2460 Vine Street since September, but her sister has been there for several years.

Giải thích:

live in a building → không gian lớn

at + địa chỉ cụ thể

since + mốc thời gian

for + khoảng thời gian

📌 Cặp sát thủ TOEIC:

since ≠ for (đừng nhầm!)

10. The oil industry is ________ far the most important industry ________ Saudi Arabia.

✅ Đáp án: by – in

👉 Hoàn chỉnh câu:

The oil industry is by far the most important industry in Saudi Arabia.

Giải thích:

by far = vượt xa, rất nhiều (trạng ngữ nhấn mạnh)

in Saudi Arabia → quốc gia

📌 Cụm “ăn điểm” TOEIC:

by far + so sánh nhất
